A little privacy, please.

Puppy Pals stores your level progress, preferences and free-play information on your device. It does not use advertising trackers or analytics.

Your progress

Progress is saved in your browser’s local storage. Clearing browser data or switching devices can remove that progress. Offline play stays on your device.

Lives and purchases

When you play online, an essential session cookie identifies your game wallet. The server stores its life balance, attempt history and purchase receipts. If you purchase extra attempts, PayPal processes the payment. Puppy Pals does not receive or store your card details. PayPal’s own privacy policy applies to its checkout.

Your choices

Purchases are optional. Free lives refill over time. You can clear local game data through your browser settings. Before clearing your session cookie, keep your PayPal receipt: paid attempts are linked to that browser’s wallet and do not currently transfer automatically to another device.
