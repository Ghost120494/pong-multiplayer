A little privacy, please.

Puppy Pals stores your level progress, preferences and free-play information on your device. It does not use advertising trackers or analytics.

Your progress

Your completed levels, stars, coins, boosters and current game save automatically on your device, using local storage with a backup copy and an IndexedDB mirror. Offline play stays on your device. Save & restore lets you download a backup file or restore one on another device. These files contain game progress, not payment credentials or purchased attempts. Clearing all browser data can remove your progress unless you keep a downloaded backup. There is no automatic cloud sync across devices.

Lives and purchases

When you play online, an essential session cookie identifies your game wallet. The server stores its life balance, attempt history and purchase receipts. Payment setup is pending, so extra-attempt purchases are currently unavailable. If enabled for the web edition, PayPal processes the payment. Puppy Pals does not receive or store your card details. PayPal’s own privacy policy applies to its checkout.

Your choices

Purchases are optional. Free lives refill over time. You can clear local game data through your browser settings. Before clearing your session cookie, keep your PayPal receipt: paid attempts are linked to that browser’s wallet and do not currently transfer automatically to another device.
